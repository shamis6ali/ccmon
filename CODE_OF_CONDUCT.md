# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. A review comment is about
  the change, not the author.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and move on.
- Remember that everyone here is doing this around other obligations.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/shamis6ali). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block accounts, in proportion to the behaviour. Serious or repeated violations
lead to a permanent ban.
